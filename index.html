About Leonardo

Leonardo M. Scarcia is an Argentine visual artist based in Santa Catarina, Brazil.

His professional background includes visual art, graphic design, illustration, art direction, animation, motion graphics, 3D and digital image production.

+ Artist Biography

Leonardo M. Scarcia began exhibiting his work publicly in 1994, participating in the Bienal de Arte Joven in Buenos Aires.

Throughout the 1990s and 2000s, he continued exhibiting and developing his artistic practice while also building a professional career in graphic design, advertising, illustration, animation and art direction.

His professional work expanded into motion graphics, audiovisual production, 3D, digital illustration, interactive experiences and visual storytelling.

Since 2021, Scarcia has also developed an active practice within digital art and the NFT ecosystem, using Web3 as another channel for exhibiting and distributing his work internationally.

His work has been presented through international digital art events and platforms, including NFT NYC, NFC Lisboa, Solana Film Festival, NFT Tianguis, Japan Metaverse Exhibition and other digital art initiatives.

Today, his practice combines physical and digital artworks, painting, animation, moving image, 3D experimentation and independent artistic projects developed through Buda Studio.

+ Professional Background

Over more than two decades, Leonardo has worked across graphic design, art direction, motion graphics, video editing, visual storytelling and digital production.

His professional experience includes work for brands, agencies, independent projects and multidisciplinary teams, combining concept development, design, animation and visual communication.

This background continues to inform his artistic practice, particularly in the areas of image construction, animation, composition, movement and digital media.
